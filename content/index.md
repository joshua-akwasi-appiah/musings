# Musings

Welcome. This is my digital garden: a place for notes, questions, experiments and ideas that are still taking shape.

My CV tells you some of what I have done. Musings is a place to see how I think, learn, question, test ideas and make connections.

It is not a finished collection. Some notes will be polished. Others will be rough, incomplete or still changing. That is intentional.

I work in learning and development, mentor young men and contribute to public engagement around clean air. I am interested in how people learn, how they work together and how ideas can become useful in the real world.

## Explore the garden

- [[work-and-practice|Work & Practice]]: reflections from learning, leadership, communication and organisational life.
- [[learning|Learning]]: notes from books, articles, conversations, courses and experience.
- [[ideas-and-observations|Ideas & Observations]]: questions and thoughts about people, work, culture, technology and society.
- [[experiments|Experiments]]: things I am trying, what happened and what I learned.
- [[people-and-public-engagement|People & Public Engagement]]: reflections from mentoring, communication and public engagement.
