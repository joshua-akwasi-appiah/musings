# About

I am Joshua Akwasi Appiah.

I enjoy learning, exploring ideas, experimenting and trying new things. I like taking an idea seriously enough to see what happens when it is put into practice.

I currently work in learning and development, a field I care deeply about. I believe that what people learn can open doors. It shapes the opportunities they can access and the ways they are able to live and work. I am interested in creating learning programmes that do more than transfer information: programmes that help people think differently, grow in confidence and make meaningful changes in how they work.

My work includes content development, learning design and creating space for useful learning conversations. I am especially interested in the connection between learning, behaviour, communication, leadership and organisational culture.

I also mentor a group of young men. I care about helping young people recognise their purpose, take responsibility for their lives and see beyond the limits of their immediate circumstances.

Alongside this, I work in public engagement with Clean Air One Atmosphere. This has deepened my interest in making complex ideas understandable and helping people connect information to the lives they are already living.

I hold a degree in Theatre and Playwriting, and an MSc in Communication and International Marketing.

## Why Musings exists

Musings is where I make room for ideas that are still developing.

It is not a portfolio or a polished account of everything I have done. It is a place to follow questions, record experiments, reflect on work and learning, and connect ideas that might otherwise stay separate.

Some notes will be finished. Others will be rough. Both belong here.
