## The challenge

The programme was designed to help commercial teams move beyond describing services and have stronger conversations about the operational, financial and client outcomes those services support.

## What I designed

I developed a 14-week sequence around commercial capability, client economics, property intelligence, prospecting, discovery, pricing, winning work, handover, retention and account expansion.

The design placed greater emphasis on applied practice. Sessions were built around real commercial situations, reflection on weekly action and clearer connections between service delivery and client value.

## Confidentiality

The organisation and its internal materials remain private. This case study focuses on the learning-design challenge and my contribution rather than client information or results.
