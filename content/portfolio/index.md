# Portfolio

I work across learning, communication and human behaviour. I help turn organisational challenges and complex ideas into content and learning experiences that people can understand, discuss and apply.

My work is not limited to one job title. Before moving directly into learning and development, I developed related experience by preparing product explainers, translating technical information for non-technical audiences and delivering presentations to stakeholders. Those experiences continue to shape how I approach learning content today: begin with what people need to understand, make it clear and give them something useful to do with it.

Today, I am Content Lead at Forums@Work, where I develop learning programmes, stories, discussion questions, facilitation materials and practical frameworks. I also facilitate peer-learning forum sessions, both internally and with external groups.
