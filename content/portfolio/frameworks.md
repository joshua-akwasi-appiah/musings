## Developed for learning programmes

<div class="framework-grid">
  <div><span>01</span><h3>Adjusting Authority</h3><p>A way to think about when leaders should provide direction and when they should create room for others to act.</p></div><div><span>02</span><h3>Clarity and Confidence</h3><p>An approach to making expectations clear enough for people to act with confidence.</p></div><div><span>03</span><h3>Decision Review</h3><p>A structured reflection on how a decision was made and what can improve next time.</p></div><div><span>04</span><h3>Leadership and Trust</h3><p>A prompt for examining how leadership behaviour shapes trust, ownership and team confidence.</p></div><div><span>05</span><h3>Recognition</h3><p>A practical approach to making recognition specific, timely and meaningful.</p></div><div><span>06</span><h3>Empowered Action</h3><p>A model for helping people move from hesitation to responsible action.</p></div><div><span>07</span><h3>Four-Step Tension</h3><p>A way to recognise and work through tension before it becomes harder to address.</p></div><div><span>08</span><h3>Safety Signals Guide</h3><p>A guide to noticing the conditions that make it easier or harder for people to speak up.</p></div><div><span>09</span><h3>Naming the Elephant</h3><p>A prompt for bringing an important but unspoken issue into an honest conversation.</p></div><div><span>10</span><h3>Mirror Standard</h3><p>A reflection on whether the standards we ask of others are visible in our own behaviour.</p></div><div><span>11</span><h3>Ownership Ladder</h3><p>A practical way to talk about the movement from waiting for direction to taking ownership.</p></div>

## Adapted and established approaches

I also adapt and use established ideas where they help learners make sense of their work. These include the SBI feedback model, the Three Zones of Listening, the Cap, Haircut, Tattoo framework, the Decision Level Pyramid and the Issue Plan framework. Their use here is about thoughtful application, not ownership.

<p class="framework-note">The public descriptions on this page are intentionally brief. They explain the purpose of the approaches without reproducing proprietary learning materials.</p>
