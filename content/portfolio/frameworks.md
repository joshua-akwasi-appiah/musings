## Adapted and established approaches

I also adapt and use established ideas where they help learners make sense of their work. These include the SBI feedback model, the Three Zones of Listening, the Cap, Haircut, Tattoo framework, the Decision Level Pyramid and the Issue Clearing framework. Their use here is about thoughtful application, not ownership.

<p class="framework-note">The public descriptions on this page are intentionally brief. They explain the purpose of the approaches without reproducing proprietary learning materials.</p>
