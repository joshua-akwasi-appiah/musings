## The starting point

Culture becomes real in everyday moments: the quality of work people accept, whether they wait to be asked, how they respond when a standard slips and whether they think about the people affected by their work.

## What I designed

I designed a short forum programme around self-leadership, taking initiative, holding team standards and working beyond one’s own lane. Each week used reflection, discussion and daily action to connect culture with specific behaviour.

## The approach

Rather than treating culture as a slogan, the programme asked participants to notice friction points, name standards and act on issues before they became someone else’s problem.
