## The work

I designed the sequence and learning content for this program. It brought together stories, questions, action steps, surveys and facilitator materials to help participants explore how safety, authority and trust shape everyday behaviour at work.

## The learning journey

The program moved from the conditions that make honest conversation possible to practical questions of decision-making, issue clearing, leadership and team empowerment. It was designed to make psychological safety concrete: not simply feeling comfortable, but having the clarity and trust to raise concerns, take responsibility and learn from mistakes.

## Design principle

The program uses established ideas where they are useful, alongside original sequencing and program design. The aim was always practical application, not simply introducing a concept.
