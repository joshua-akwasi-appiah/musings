## The challenge

Ownership is often described as an attitude, but it becomes meaningful through observable choices: taking initiative, owning mistakes, giving honest feedback, understanding the business and following through.

## What I designed

I designed a 14-week sequence that gave those choices a practical rhythm. Each week combined a learning theme, survey questions, forum discussion and an action step that participants could test in their everyday work.

Themes included employee and owner mindsets, personal initiative, values, focus, ownership of mistakes, candid feedback, financial literacy, decision-making, role fit and naming difficult issues.

## Evidence and next steps

The programme included surveys, action steps and before-and-after questions so that behaviour could be examined over time. A fuller impact account will be added once the relevant survey and participant evidence has been reviewed.
