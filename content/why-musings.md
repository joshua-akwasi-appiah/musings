---
title: Why Musings
description: Why Joshua Akwasi Appiah keeps a digital garden.
---

I think a lot. I imagine most people do.

A thought can begin with a conversation, something I have read, a film, a song, an observation at work or a question that stays with me longer than I expected.

Thoughts matter because they shape how we see the world, how we understand people, how we make decisions and how we notice what might be possible.

Musings is a place to stay with some of those thoughts for longer.

It is where I can share what I am learning, test ideas, question assumptions, revisit beliefs and make connections between things that may not seem related at first.

I hope it also becomes a place for thoughtful exchange. A place where you may find an idea worth sitting with, disagree honestly, make a connection or bring a thought of your own.
